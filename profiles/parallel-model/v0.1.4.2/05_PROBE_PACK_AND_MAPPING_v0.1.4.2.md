# 05 — Probe Pack and Mapping v0.1.4.2

Status: **CANDIDATE PROBE PACK**

Probe count/families are preserved. Compression changes ownership/mapping, not accident fixtures.

# 1. Probe classes

- **MECHANICAL** — durable identities/state/gates.
- **ORACLE** — parent-only semantic truth.
- **BENCHMARK** — predeclared optimization objective/threshold.

Safety verdict and efficiency characterization remain separate.
